Trials of The Third Business Data Analysis (Business Data Analysis and AI Application Direction)

May 2025 – Jun 2025

  • Utilized a combined methodology of data collection, cleaning, mining, machine learning, and agent development on a real e-commerce dataset provided by the competition; applied Excel, BI tools, and AI platforms to conduct multi-dimensional business data analysis and construct intelligent applications.
  • Analyzed corporate market landscape, product competitiveness, store operational health, and consumer behavior patterns; extracted data insights through clustering, prediction, and market basket analysis models; built an AI-powered customer service chatbot to enhance business decision-making efficiency and automation.
  • Findings demonstrated that data analysis and AI applications significantly improve operational insight and decision-making accuracy, providing solutions and references for corporate digital transformation in e-commerce.

Project: Research on the Impact of AIGC Usage on Knowledge Dissemination Effectiveness Based on the SOR Model

Feb 2025 – May 2025

  • Targeted youth aged 14-35 in Guangdong Province, analyzing the relationship between AIGC usage, the new digital divide, and knowledge dissemination effects based on the SOR model.

Project: Research on the Influence Mechanism of Consumer Willingness to Purchase Fruit via Live Streaming

Jan 2024 – Jan 2025

  • Constructed a model of factors based on the Stimulus-Organism-Response (S-O-R) theory.
  • Designed and distributed questionnaires (N=1000+); validated hypotheses using SEM; research outcomes were adopted by county-level e-commerce initiatives.
  • Proposed "segmented marketing strategies" incorporating K-means clustering to improve conversion rates.
  • Completed relevant research reports; aligned findings with live streaming sales practices under the "Hundreds, Thousands, Tens of Thousands Project", providing theoretical support and practical solutions for fruit farmers to increase agricultural product sales and support rural revitalization.
National College Student Entrepreneurship Competition

Project: Influence Mechanism and Effects of Green Economy on High-Quality Energy Development under "Dual Carbon" Goals

Apr 2024 – Apr 2025

  • Conducted literature review, inductive summary, and empirical analysis; utilized data from 30 provinces (2015-2021) and the entropy weight method to measure green economy and high-quality energy development.
  • Analyzed the direct effect of green economy on high-quality energy development, the mediating effect of technological progress, the moderating role of constraining government behavior, as well as its spatial spillover effects and regional heterogeneity.
  • Results indicated that the green economy significantly promotes high-quality energy development with observable spatial spillover effects and regional heterogeneity, providing policy recommendations for energy transition and sustainable development.
National Collegiate Statistical Modeling Contest

Project: Impact of Officials' Tenure on Corporate Environmental Performance

Mar 2024 – Jul 2024 · First Prize, Guangdong Province

  • Constructed panel regression models based on data from prefecture-level city officials and listed companies (2010-2020) to explore government-enterprise environmental linkage mechanisms.
  • Empirical findings revealed a U-shaped relationship between tenure length and corporate environmental performance, moderated by market competition and public supervision.
  • Proposed a government-led, multi-stakeholder collaborative governance solution, offering theoretical support and policy pathways for the coordinated development of the economy and the environment.
